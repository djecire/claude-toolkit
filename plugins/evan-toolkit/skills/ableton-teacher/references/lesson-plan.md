# Lesson plan

Eight short lessons in this order; each depends on the one before. Evan says when to start and may ask to jump ahead. Progress lives in auto-memory (the ableton-teaching note). Update it after every lesson.

Each lesson below lists: goal, what to teach, steps, the task, the check (evidence to ask for) and the mistakes to watch for. The detailed methods live in warping.md, stems-and-keys.md and recipes.md.

## Lesson 1: Orientation (10 min)

Goal: find his way around and hear sound.

Teach: two views of the same tracks (Tab); the Browser; Info View (Shift+?); Settings → Audio; adding his music folder.

Steps:
1. Press Tab a few times and watch Session and Arrangement swap.
2. Shift+? opens Info View (bottom left). Hover five controls and read what it says.
3. ⌘, → Audio → Output Device = headphones or interface. Buffer Size 256 or 512.
4. Browser → Places → Add Folder… → his music folder.
5. Drag a song into Arrangement View and press Space.

Task: play a song through his speakers.

Check: did sound come out; what Info View said when he hovered the tempo box.

Mistakes: no sound = wrong Output Device or the Main fader is down; Browser missing = ⌘⌥B brings it back.

## Lesson 2: Warping (20 min)

Goal: lock two songs of different tempos to one BPM.

Teach: what warp markers are, Set 1.1.1 Here, checking with the metronome, Complex Pro. See warping.md Methods A and B.

Task: two songs both at 124 BPM, each staying on the click for 32 bars, then both playing together.

Check: the BPM box value for each song after warping; whether the click stays locked at bar 1 and bar 32; optional screenshot of the marker strip.

Mistakes: starting on a drumless intro; tempo detected at half or double (use ÷2 or ×2); Beats mode on a full song.

## Lesson 3: Arrangement editing (20 min)

Goal: cut a song up and rebuild it, the way an extended intro or DJ edit is made.

Teach: selecting time; ⌘E split; ⌘D duplicate; ⌘J consolidate; ⌘L loop; F shows fade handles and ⌘⌥F creates fades; locators (Set button in the scrub area, ⌘R to rename); ⌘I insert silence; 0 deactivate; Z/X zoom to selection; H/W fit.

Task: a 32-bar intro edit: 16 bars of a drums-only loop from the song's own intro or breakdown, then the original from its first drop, with a fade at the join.

Check: the bar number of the join; whether it plays through without a click or a gap.

Mistakes: grid snapping turned off by accident (⌘4); edits not on bar lines; loop brace left in the wrong place.

## Lesson 4: Stems and the first mashup (30 min)

Goal: an acapella over another song's instrumental, in key.

Teach: Separate Stems; deactivating stems with 0; Complex Pro with Formants; Transpose; the Camelot check. See stems-and-keys.md and recipes.md section 1.

Task: 16 bars of song A's vocal over song B's instrumental, in key, with EQ Eight cutting the vocal's lows.

Check: both Camelot codes and the transpose amount he used; whether the chorus sounds in tune.

Mistakes: a vocal 4 or more semitones away (choose a different pairing); High Speed stems on an exposed vocal.

## Lesson 5: Transitions and automation (25 min)

Goal: move between two sections or two songs without a bump.

Teach: A shows automation; one lane per parameter; EQ Three bass swap; Auto Filter sweep; an echo throw on a send; the drop-out; a reverse hit. See recipes.md section 2.

Task: a 16-bar transition from song A into song B using a bass swap and one filter sweep.

Check: the bar where the bass swaps; a screenshot of the automation lanes.

Mistakes: automation drawn on the wrong lane; two basslines overlapping; fades on the wrong clip.

## Lesson 6: DJ mix (30 min)

Goal: a three-song mix built from a reusable template.

Teach: the two-deck template (EQ Three and Utility on each deck, a MASTER OUT track, Limiter on Main); headroom; clip gain; Song Tempo automation; saving as a template. See recipes.md section 5.

Task: build the template, save it, then mix three songs with two blends.

Check: the template shows under User Library → Templates; the Main meter's peak during the loudest blend (it should stay out of the red).

Mistakes: decks not routed to MASTER OUT; Limiter placed before the effects bus; clips not phrase-aligned (count in 8s).

## Lesson 7: Wordplay edit (25 min)

Goal: a lyric-led switch between two songs.

Teach: locators as cue points; splitting at the word (⌘E); looping the incoming instrumental stem; a delay throw on the last word; Simpler Slice for word chops. See recipes.md section 3.

Task: one wordplay switch, exported as a one-minute clip.

Check: which two lyrics link, and the bar where the switch lands.

Mistakes: the switch landing off the downbeat; the vocal too quiet under the throw.

## Lesson 8: Bootleg and finishing (40 min)

Goal: a bootleg of one song with its own drums, loudness-matched and exported.

Teach: splitting and colouring sections; deactivating the original drums and bass; new drums from breaks and one-shots; reverse-snare fills; Limiter and Utility for loudness; Spectrum to compare lows; ⌘⇧R export; Collect All and Save. See recipes.md sections 4 and 6.

Task: a two-minute bootleg exported as WAV and MP3.

Check: the export settings he used; the Main peak level; the file plays in his DJ software at the right BPM.

Mistakes: Normalize ticked by accident; forgetting Collect All and Save before closing.

## Optional Lesson 9: Session View and the crossfader (20 min)

For live performance: clips and scenes; launch quantization; the A/B crossfader (View → Mixer Controls); Cue Out to a second output for headphone cueing; MIDI Map (⌘M) to a controller.
